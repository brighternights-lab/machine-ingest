# Permanent Lighting Manufacturers: Dealer Website Programs, Marketing Assets, Flagship Sites, and Dealer Economics (Trimlight, Gemstone, JellyFish, EverLights, Oelo)

Research date 2026-09-28. US focus. Sources are manufacturer pages, dealer pages, franchise directories, and industry blogs. Where a figure comes from a competitor's comparison page (dfwledlite.com) it is flagged as adversarial.

## Key Question 1: What does a manufacturer supplied dealer site include and what does it lack?

### Takeaway
No manufacturer gives dealers a true standalone website. Trimlight and Gemstone give dealers a location page on the manufacturer domain (trimlight.com/locations/[city], gemstonelights.com/dealers/[dealer]) with a quote form that routes to the dealer. JellyFish lists corporate offices and "400+ locations" with all leads routed through corporate. Independent dealer sites (trimlightdallas.com, trimlightoc.com) exist but are dealer built or agency built on a shared look, and they never publish pricing or financing partner names.

### Cited Findings
- Trimlight dealer directory groups dealers by state/city with URL pattern trimlight.com/locations/[city-name] (e.g. /locations/anchorage, /locations/phoenix, /locations/san-diego); claims "330+ authorized dealers" who are "local, certified, factory-trained"; search by postal code; "No standalone dealer microsites are evident; all use the main trimlight.com domain with location-specific paths" — [Trimlight Dealer Locations](https://trimlight.com/dealer-locations)
- Trimlight Phoenix location page contains: professional installation overview with lifetime warranty, lighting solutions gallery (Holiday, Security, Accent, Globe, Patio, Pool, Game Day, Downlighting, Landscape), local context copy (Spring Training, First Fridays), energy efficiency copy, 10+ testimonials, dealer phone (602) 699-4191, address, hours, service area list (Carefree, Cave Creek, Glendale, Laveen, New River, Peoria, Phoenix), embedded quote form. No pricing, no financing details, no link to an independent dealer site — [Trimlight Phoenix](https://trimlight.com/locations/phoenix)
- Trimlight Dallas (independent domain trimlightdallas.com, Frisco TX) nav: Lighting (accent, holiday, security, down, commercial, landscape, game day, ridge), Gallery, Reviews, About, Contact; footer Troubleshooting, Warranty, Financing, Sitemap. Pricing "not publicly listed" ("exact cost varies based on size and scope of your project (i.e. linear footage, number and size of peaks, turrets, rental equipment needed etc.)"). Financing: partners with "specialized finance companies" but no lender named. App claims "over 180 presets, pre-configured holiday patterns and daily timers." Also HOA compliance rep, military/first responder/teacher discounts — [Trimlight Dallas FAQ](https://trimlightdallas.com/faq/)
- Trimlight Orange County (trimlightoc.com) nav: Products (Accent, Holiday, Security, Globe, Patio, Game Day, Downlighting), Residential/Commercial/Municipal, About (3L, Classic, Commercial bulb, Pricing, FAQ), Gallery, Get a Quote. Quote form: name, email, phone, address, question. Cites "285 authorized dealers nationwide" and lifetime product warranty. Serves Orange County, Riverside County, Inland Empire. "No pricing details, financing partners, or app name are disclosed." Assessed as a "branded dealership template distributed to authorized dealers" (repeating nav, generic form, dealer-agnostic copy) — [Trimlight OC](https://trimlightoc.com/)
- Gemstone dealer microsite (gemstonelights.com/dealers/brilliant-solutions) includes: Gemstone-branded header/nav, dealer name, address, "Since 2019," social links and link to dealer's own website, phone; "96% satisfaction rating with 344 surveys"; "411 Jobs Completed in Last 12 Months"; badges (Licensed Electrician, Master Installation Badge, Offers Financing, Office Building); customer reviews with metric breakdown (Dealer Professionalism 97.5%, Customer Product Training 93.78%, Work Site Cleanliness 96.4%); 2 before/after images plus ~19 install photos; 6 FAQs; quote form (property type, name, company, email, phone, address, notes, access consent); Get Directions. Lacks: service area definition, pricing, warranty detail, install timeline, financing explanation, team bios, video, competitor comparison, promotions — [Gemstone dealer page Brilliant Solutions](https://www.gemstonelights.com/dealers/brilliant-solutions/)
- Gemstone city landing page (Fort Worth) includes: local hero copy, "Our Journey" (founded 2016 by Tate Leavitt, Calgary, "over 300 markets across North America"), local gallery, Vimeo Hub app demo, two certified dealers listed (Lighting by GGA DFW, Vertex Outdoor Lighting), quote form with optional dealer preference and SMS consent, pricing statement ("depends on two factors: the required linear footage and the prevailing local market rate"), operating cost example (117 ft / 150 lights, 6 hrs/day = about $4.34/month), FAQ (RGBWW, 46 lumens per light), product categories (Residential Track, Commercial Track, Flood), suburb list. No financing — [Gemstone Fort Worth](https://www.gemstonelights.com/location/usa/texas/permanent-christmas-lights-in-fort-worth-tx/)
- JellyFish "Where to Buy" lists 8 corporate offices (Utah, Colorado, Texas, Florida) plus "400+ US locations" by state/city; "no evidence of franchisees or dealers receiving their own branded websites or dedicated pages. All inquiries route through the centralized corporate contact system." Form fields: first/last name, email, phone, zip, street, city, state, lighting type dropdown; "Please fill out the form to be contacted by the closest location." Phone/SMS 801.613.7421 — [JellyFish Find a Dealer](https://jellyfishlighting.com/find-a-dealer)
- Trimlight dealer knowledge base ("Where do I get downloadable files and other digital help?") is login-gated members-only; content not public — [Trimlight Access](https://trimlight.com/access/where-to-start-as-a-trimlight-dealer)
- EverLights dealer support page is purely technical (Bridge, App, Wireless Receiver, Lights, Warranty, Custom Installation), dealer portal at portal.myEverLights.com/app, phone 385.233.0220; no marketing assets, website templates, or financing partners listed — [EverLights Dealer Support](https://myeverlights.com/dealer-support/)
- Oelo requires an "Oelo web page" for Authorized, Preferred, and Platinum dealers (a listing on oelo.com, not a standalone site); leads "delegated to area's highest-level dealer" — [Oelo Dealer Tiers PDF](https://www.oelo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/OeloDealerTiers2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- The manufacturer supplied "site" is really a lead-capture location page on the manufacturer domain. Dealers do not own the domain, the SEO, or the form, and the manufacturer controls lead routing (Oelo and Gemstone explicitly route to the highest-tier or preferred dealer).
- What is universally missing from manufacturer supplied dealer pages: published pricing, named financing partner, online booking/scheduling, instant estimate tools, dealer team/brand identity, video, and service-area pages for SEO.
- Independent Trimlight dealer domains (trimlight[city].com) are dealer-owned sites on a shared design; the strongest ones add Financing, Warranty, Reviews, Gallery, and HOA pages, but still hide price.
- Gemstone's dealer page is the richest manufacturer-supplied format because it exposes survey-verified satisfaction scores and 12-month job counts, which a competing partner program could match with a public trust score.

### Gaps
- Could not view the contents of Trimlight's login-gated dealer knowledge base (asset library, website help) or the JellyFish dealer hub (dealerhub.jellyfishlighting.com).
- Did not find a public JellyFish "authorized dealer page" template; JellyFish appears to route everything through corporate, and franchise.com states "JellyFish Lighting is not currently accepting inquiries."
- Could not confirm whether Trimlight corporate builds trimlight[city].com sites for dealers or dealers build them (the OC site looks templated but no source states who builds it).

## Key Question 2: What do the flagship sites do that dealer sites do not?

### Takeaway
Flagship sites carry the product depth (bulb variants, app feature lists, patterns, smart-home integrations), trust proof at scale (Trustpilot/Google review counts, dealer counts, homeowner counts), AI visualizers, dealer verification, warranty registration, and centralized quote routing. Dealer pages inherit the brand but rarely the tools.

### Cited Findings
- Trimlight.com nav: Solutions (Holiday incl. Christmas, Halloween, July 4th, Easter, St. Patrick's, Mardi Gras, New Year's; Accent; Down; Game Day; Globe; Landscape; Patio; Pool Cages; Security), Residential/Commercial/Municipal, About (3L Bulb, Classic Bulb, Commercial Bulb, Blog), plus Get a Quote, Repair Request, Support, Request a Brochure, FAQ, Become a Dealer, Edge App. Warranty: "Lifetime product warranty on core components" up to 50,000 hours, replacement parts through local dealers, "no ladders or DIY repairs required." Claims "Over 300 authorized dealerships nationwide." No financing partner named — [Trimlight.com](https://trimlight.com/)
- Trimlight quote entry is a dealer locator ("Get a Free Quote") at trimlight.com/dealer-locator — [Trimlight Dealer Locator](https://www.trimlight.com/dealer-locator)
- Trimlight comparison blog claims Trimlight offers "one lifetime warranty on all parts" covering "the wiring, diodes, channel, and controller" and three spacing options (6, 9, 12 in) vs Gemstone fixed 9 in — [Trimlight vs Gemstone blog](https://trimlight.com/blog/gemstone-comparrison)
- Gemstonelights.com: product lines Permanent Outdoor (track) Lighting and Landscape Lighting; occasion pages (Architectural, Christmas, NYE, Valentine's, Halloween, Canada Day, 4th of July, Security, Gameday); app features (26+ animations, 16M+ colors, Music Mode, Autopilot, pattern creation, dimmable warm white, zones, sunset/sunrise timer); Hub and Hub2 apps ("6000+ reviews, 4.9 rating"); "Free AI Preview/Visualizer tool"; Gallery; Testimonials ("4.9 Trustpilot rating, 950+ reviews"); Quick Start Guide; Warranty Registration; "Verify Your Dealer"; Become a Dealer; Blogs; FAQs. Quote flow: free quote form, authorized dealer connection, home measurement, professional install. Financing not detailed — [Gemstone Lights](https://www.gemstonelights.com/)
- Gemstone warranty: "Lifetime warranty on the lights" (50,000 hrs, ~20 yrs at 7 hrs/day), "10-year warranty for all products and parts" (power kits, controllers, cables, connectors, tracks), one-year labor, registration within 30 days required, transferable once within the 10-year period for up to 2 years if transferred within 90 days of possession — [Gemstone Warranty](https://www.gemstonelights.com/warranty/)
- Gemstone dealer portal (beta.gemstonelights.com/portal) and GS Dealer Portal Android app exist — [Gemstone Portal](https://beta.gemstonelights.com/portal/); [GS Dealer Portal app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gemstone.dealer&hl=en_CA&gl=US)

Trust: "4.7 Google rating, 1,300+ reviews," "Trusted by 50,000+ homeowners since 2016." No warranty terms, financing partner, or visualizer found on the homepage — [JellyFish Lighting](https://jellyfishlighting.com/)

- Oelo flagship claims: five-year product warranty, 100,000 hour rated lifespan (~34 years), U.S. Pat. No. 10,006,615, "Average residential install: $5,000+" — [Oelo Franchise Opportunities](https://www.oelo.com/outdoor-lighting-franchise-opportunities/)

### Inferences
- Flagship-only capabilities a partner program should push down to the contractor level: AI visualizer (Gemstone), dealer verification badge, warranty registration, app demo video, smart-home integration pages (JellyFish), occasion/holiday landing pages, HOA page (JellyFish), DIY page (JellyFish).
- None of the five flagship sites publicly names a consumer financing partner. Financing appears only as a dealer badge (Gemstone "Offers Financing") or generic copy (Trimlight Dallas).
- Trimlight, Gemstone and JellyFish all centralize the quote form and then assign the lead, so the dealer never controls first contact.

### Gaps
- EverLights flagship (myeverlights.com) page inventory was not fully captured; the become-a-dealer page redirected to a HubSpot ad landing page and a Shopify page, both thin.
- JellyFish's own warranty page and financing page were not fetched; only the competitor summary is available.

## Key Question 3: What pricing, financing and warranty information is shown publicly?

### Takeaway
Manufacturers never publish per-foot prices; pricing is "depends on linear footage and local rate." Warranty is the main public differentiator (Trimlight lifetime all parts, Gemstone lifetime lights + 10 yr parts + 1 yr labor, JellyFish 5 yr product + 1 yr labor per competitor, Oelo 5 yr). Financing is mentioned generically; no lender is named on any manufacturer or dealer page fetched.

### Cited Findings
- Gemstone: "The pricing of Gemstone Lights Track Lighting depends on two factors: the required linear footage and the prevailing local market rate for installation." — [Gemstone Fort Worth](https://www.gemstonelights.com/location/usa/texas/permanent-christmas-lights-in-fort-worth-tx/)
- Trimlight on Gemstone: "Gemstone Lights doesn't publish a flat per-foot rate; every quote comes from a local authorized dealer" — [Trimlight blog](https://trimlight.com/blog/gemstone-comparrison)
- Trimlight Dallas: no per-foot or average cost; financing through unnamed "specialized finance companies" — [Trimlight Dallas FAQ](https://trimlightdallas.com/faq/)
- Trimlight warranty: lifetime on core components up to 50,000 hours — [Trimlight.com](https://trimlight.com/)
- Gemstone warranty: lifetime lights, 10 yr parts, 1 yr labor, 30-day registration, limited transfer — [Gemstone Warranty](https://www.gemstonelights.com/warranty/)
- Competitor-reported Gemstone pricing: "$25-$42 per linear foot" plus "$650-$850 extra" controller; 150 ft job "$4,400-$7,150"; warranty described as "10 year labor, 5 year parts" (conflicts with Gemstone's own page above); claims Gemstone dealers "pay ongoing royalties and marketing fees" (unverified, contradicted by Gemstone's dealer page which lists no royalties) — [DFW LED vs Gemstone (adversarial)](https://dfwledlite.com/vs-gemstone)
- Competitor-reported JellyFish pricing: "$30-40/ft installed"; typical 150 to 250 ft home "$4,500-$10,000+"; 200 ft Fort Worth example ~$5,600; JellyFish warranty "1-year labor, 5-year product." DFW LED itself offers $25/ft flat, lifetime workmanship, 3 yr parts and labor, 0% in-house financing and Affirm — [DFW LED vs JellyFish (adversarial)](https://dfwledlite.com/blog/jellyfish-cost-explained)
- Oelo: five-year product warranty; average residential install $5,000+ — [Oelo Franchise](https://www.oelo.com/outdoor-lighting-franchise-opportunities/)
- Gemstone operating cost example: 117 ft, 150 lights, 6 hrs/day ≈ $4.34/month, $52.80/year — [Gemstone Fort Worth](https://www.gemstonelights.com/location/usa/texas/permanent-christmas-lights-in-fort-worth-tx/)
- Gemstone dealer badge "Offers Financing" shown on dealer page — [Gemstone dealer page](https://www.gemstonelights.com/dealers/brilliant-solutions/)
- Independent installers commonly publish a Financing page (examples: Carolina Accent, Nexwave, Happy Lites) while the manufacturer sites do not — [Carolina Accent Financing](https://www.carolinaaccentlighting.com/financing); [Nexwave Financing](https://nexwavelighting.com/financing/); [Happy Lites Financing](https://www.happylites.com/financing)

### Inferences
- Publishing a transparent per-foot range and a named financing partner on partner sites would be a visible differentiator, since every incumbent hides both. The DFW LED pages show that "transparent flat $/ft + 0% financing" is already being used as an attack angle against JellyFish and Gemstone.
- Warranty transferability and labor coverage are the fine print competitors attack; a partner program that bundles a longer labor warranty (dealer funded or manufacturer backed) would beat Gemstone's 1 year and JellyFish's reported 1 year.

### Gaps
- No manufacturer names its financing partner (Wisetack, GreenSky, Synchrony, Affirm) on the pages fetched; the search returned only third-party installers.
- JellyFish and EverLights official warranty pages not fetched.

## Key Question 4: What are published average job values and per foot prices for permanent lighting?

### Takeaway
Industry sources converge on $20 to $40 per linear foot installed and an average residential job of roughly $4,000 to $8,000, with Strandr citing a $7,500 average permanent install and Oelo citing $5,000+. Dealer net margins are described as 30 to 50 percent.

### Cited Findings
- "$20–$30 per linear foot installed" standard US rate; small home (100 ft) $2,000–$3,000; medium (150–200 ft) $3,000–$6,000; large (250–400 ft) $6,000–$12,000; average homeowner spends $4,000–$8,000; cost split materials 30–40%, labor 20–30%, overhead/marketing 10–15%, net profit 30–50%; example 200 ft at $25/ft = $5,000 revenue, $2,000 gross profit — [Thunder Lighting Supply](https://thunderlightingsupply.com/how-much-do-permanent-lights-cost/)
- Basic single-color "$15-$25 per linear foot," premium smart RGB "$25-$40 per linear foot"; typical permanent install averages "$7,500" vs "$1,200" seasonal; 250 ft roofline $3,750–$6,250 basic or $6,250–$10,000 premium; "$3,000" minimum project; maintenance contracts "$200-$500 per customer," seasonal programming "$300-$800 per season"; contractor with 100 seasonal accounts at $120,000/yr could reach "$344,000" by adding permanent — [Strandr contractor guide](https://www.strandr.com/blog/permanent-lighting-year-round-revenue-contractor-guide)
- Oelo: "Average residential install: $5,000+" — [Oelo Franchise](https://www.oelo.com/outdoor-lighting-franchise-opportunities/)
- Competitor-reported: JellyFish $30–40/ft, Gemstone $25–42/ft plus controller — [DFW LED JellyFish](https://dfwledlite.com/blog/jellyfish-cost-explained); [DFW LED Gemstone](https://dfwledlite.com/vs-gemstone)
- Trimlight legacy dealer flyer (undated, references trimlight.net) required each dealer to buy a minimum 400 linear feet of channel per zip code per year, grow 10% yearly, and spend $200/year per zip on local advertising — [Trimlight dealership flyer PDF](https://assets.website-files.com/605a4cb28f3348ce9b1132c9/612eba943a3b564d6fbaacf5_dealership_flyer_2033.pdf)
- Gemstone dealer Brilliant Solutions (Denver) shows "411 Jobs Completed in Last 12 Months" — [Gemstone dealer page](https://www.gemstonelights.com/dealers/brilliant-solutions/)

### Inferences
- At a $5,000 to $7,500 average ticket, a Gemstone dealer doing 411 jobs/yr implies roughly $2M to $3M in annual permanent lighting revenue for a top dealer (inference from two sources, not a published figure).
- Oelo's tier commitments ($25K–$75K+ annual product purchases) and Trimlight's $5K–$10K entry imply that manufacturers size a starting dealer at roughly 10 to 30 jobs in year one.

### Gaps
- No manufacturer publishes average dealer revenue, average job size (except Oelo's "$5,000+"), or per-foot pricing. Trimlight's become-a-dealer page shows placeholder stats ("0 active dealers, 0% margin") where economics would sit.
- JellyFish franchise directories list investment only ($20,000–$25,000) with no revenue.

## Key Question 5: What dealer marketing support is promised on their "become a dealer" pages?

### Takeaway
Promised support is thin and generic: "marketing materials," a portal, training, a success manager or lighting specialist, and manufacturer-referred leads. Oelo is the only one that publishes specifics (tiered ad match up to $7,000, brochure quantities, sales match, lead priority). Nobody promises a dealer website, SEO, paid ads management, CRM, or booking tools.

### Cited Findings
- Trimlight: entry $5,000 + $499 demo kit (≤30 zip codes) or $10,000 + $499 (31+ zips), remaining funds become material credit; "Nothing! We don't take royalties"; exclusive zip-code territory; residency requirement; mandatory 2-day Salt Lake City training (installation, app, sales, marketing, troubleshooting); dedicated success manager; "ready-to-deploy playbook on day one" with manufacturer-referred leads, marketing infrastructure and materials, territory-exclusive marketing rights; lifetime warranty; wholesale pricing with volume discounts; display case and aluminum color samples; app access — [Trimlight Become a Dealer](https://trimlight.com/become-a-dealer); application at [Trimlight Apply](https://trimlight.com/become-a-dealer/apply)
- Trimlight legacy flyer packages: A $2,500, B $3,500, C $7,500, each + $60/zip code (SLC 65 zips example $6,400–$10,400); includes 1,500+ custom branded sales aids, Excel estimating software, portable sales display, 20 yard signs, 1,000 door hangers, 500 postcards (Package B), "company photos, logos, videos, national web presence" — [Trimlight flyer PDF](https://assets.website-files.com/605a4cb28f3348ce9b1132c9/612eba943a3b564d6fbaacf5_dealership_flyer_2033.pdf)
- Gemstone: "Advertising support and access to a range of marketing materials"; all-in-one dealer portal (storefront/ordering, real-time order tracking, leads management, service/claims requests, analytics dashboard, Hub2 app integration); "comprehensive training"; dedicated customer service; "competitive dealer rates"; dealer tier program with performance-based benefits. Applicant requirements: 5+ years in business, $2M+ annual revenue, employed sales staff, skilled installers, must offer financing. No territory, financing, or lead program specifics — [Gemstone Authorized Dealer Program](https://www.gemstonelights.com/the-authorized-dealer-program/); [Gemstone Dealer Opportunity](https://www.gemstonelights.com/dealer-opportunity/)
- JellyFish: "dedicated lighting specialist who is focused on your success"; guided training; dealer pricing; "Startup costs are low. There are different opening purchase levels with no added franchise or territory fees"; Dealer Kit; dealer portal dealerhub.jellyfishlighting.com; FAQ addresses "Am I buying a franchise?" and "Do you sell territories?"; no marketing, lead, website, or financing detail — [JellyFish Dealers](https://www.jellyfishlighting.com/resources/dealers); [JellyFish Become a Dealer](https://jellyfishlighting.com/become-a-dealer/)
- JellyFish via franchise.com: minimum cash $20,000, total investment $20,000–$25,000; "onsite training on both installation and sales and marketing strategy"; "comprehensive Dealer Starter Kit"; "We'll work with you to generate traffic and leads through social media, web, and inbound calls"; "JellyFish Lighting is not currently accepting inquiries" — [Franchise.com JellyFish](https://www.franchise.com/franchise/jellyfish-lighting)
- EverLights: "Access the best training, marketing resources, technical assistance, and sales tools in the market"; "minimal startup costs that include product to turn a profit from the outset"; no specialty tools required; page is an appointment-driving HubSpot landing page with no program specifics — [EverLights dealer landing page](https://myeverlights-4437302.hs-sites.com/dealer-acquisition-landing-page_ads)
- Oelo tiers: Contractor 10% off, no fee, online training only, no leads; Authorized 20% off, $5,000 initiation + $20,000 product credit, $25K–$74,999 annual commitment, up to $2,500 ad match, 150 brochures included, leads included (delegated to highest-tier dealer), Oelo web page required, one on-site training; Preferred 30% off, $75K+ annual, up to $5,000 ad match, HQ training with $1,000 travel allowance, free demo display, payment terms after 1 year; Platinum 35% off, $5,000 + $70,000 product credit, up to $7,000 ad match, 300 brochures, $500 apparel credit, lead priority, $2,000 travel allowance, must sell Oelo exclusively. Sales match all tiers above Contractor: $500 at $50K, $1,000 at $100K, $2,000 at $200K prior-year purchases. Online Marketing Hub with downloadable customizable materials; "FREE promotional literature"; password-protected inventory system — [Oelo Dealer Tiers PDF](https://www.oelo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/OeloDealerTiers2024.pdf); [Oelo Franchise Opportunities](https://www.oelo.com/outdoor-lighting-franchise-opportunities/); [Oelo Dealer Tiers page](https://www.oelo.com/dealer-tiers/)

### Inferences
- The gap a Brighter Nights partner program can fill: a real contractor-owned website (own domain, SEO service-area pages, published pricing range, named financing, online booking, visualizer, reviews feed), plus published dealer economics and lead routing the contractor controls. None of the incumbents promise any of those on their public dealer pages.
- Gemstone's $2M revenue and 5-year requirement locks out new entrants; Trimlight and JellyFish target them with $5K–$25K entry, but only Trimlight sells exclusive territory. A program with no territory fee, no minimum purchase, and full launch package is positioned between them.
- Oelo's ad match model ($2,500–$7,000) is the only quantified marketing co-op in the category and is a concrete benchmark to beat.

### Gaps
- Photo libraries, video libraries, and app demo assets are referenced only generically ("marketing materials," "company photos, logos, videos"); no manufacturer publicly lists the contents of its asset library. Trimlight's is behind login.
- No manufacturer publicly names a dealer-facing CRM, website builder, or lead platform. A GoHighLevel preview link appeared in search results tied to Trimlight dealer marketing but was not fetched or verified — [GoHighLevel preview link (unverified)](https://app.gohighlevel.com/v2/preview/jMBYtszjCky5YIy1fUte)
- Dealer sentiment threads (Facebook "Permanent LED Installers" group) were not accessible.
