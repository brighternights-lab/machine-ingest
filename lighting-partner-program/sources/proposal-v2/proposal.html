How it is paid for

No upfront bill. The percentage falls as you grow.

You pay a sliding percentage of your gross lighting revenue. Each tier only applies to the dollars inside it, the same way tax brackets work, so growing never bumps your whole bill into a higher rate.

Straight answers

Do I have to be a lighting contractor already?

No. Most founding partners are roofers, gutter, pool, paver, or electrical contractors adding lighting. We teach the product and the bidding.

Who owns the site while I am paying?

We do, until your credits reach the ledger. Then you do. Your domain and brand are yours from day one.

What if I leave early?

You can. The buyout is the ledger balance minus what you have already credited, plus three months of trailing fee. No other penalty.

Can I add my own pages or tools later?

Yes. We quote it in writing, you approve it, and it goes on the ledger at that price when it ships.

What do I pay before launch?

Nothing. The first invoice comes after the first month of lighting revenue.
