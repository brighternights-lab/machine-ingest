Lighting Partner Agreement

This Lighting Partner Agreement (the Agreement) is between BN Lighting Technologies, LLC, a Florida limited liability company doing business as Brighter Nights Lighting Technologies, Sanford, Florida (Brighter Nights), and the contractor on the signature page (Partner). It is effective on the date of the last signature (the Effective Date). Pages 1 through 13 are the proposal and are incorporated for context. Where they differ, this Agreement controls.

1. Definitions

Gross Lighting Revenue means every amount Partner invoices or collects, whichever comes first, for the sale, design, installation, programming, repair, or maintenance of exterior lighting and related controls for customers in the Territory, including deposits, progress payments, change orders, and financed amounts at full contract value, less only sales tax collected and amounts refunded to a customer.
Territory means the county on the signature page.
Site means the website, domain, tools, integrations, content, and accounts in Exhibit A, plus approved Add Ons.
Ledger means the dollar value of the Site under Section 5.
Credits means the amounts applied against the Ledger under Section 5.2.
Fee means the amounts due under Section 3.
Dashboard means the online portal Brighter Nights provides to Partner. Absent manifest error, the Dashboard is the record of revenue reported, Fees, Credits, the Ledger, and approvals.
Add On means any feature, page, tool, or service Partner requests in writing beyond Exhibit A.
Committed Term means the 1, 2, or 3 year term Partner elects by initialing one option on the signature page. If no option is initialed, the Committed Term is 1 year.

2. Brighter Nights obligations

Brighter Nights will design, build, host, and maintain the Site under Partner's brand and provide the ongoing services in Exhibit A. Brighter Nights will (a) make the Launch Scope live within 14 days after Partner delivers the onboarding materials in Exhibit A; (b) ship the Phase Two Scope within 90 days after launch, subject to Partner's approvals in the Dashboard; (c) keep the Site available 99.5% of each month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced in advance; (d) deploy the advertising funds in Section 4 within 60 days of each Fee payment; (e) publish a weekly report in the Dashboard; (f) respond to Partner within one business day and fix any reported Site defect within 5 business days; and (g) provide one to two hours a week of partner manager time on request. Brighter Nights may substitute a tool or vendor of equal or better function. Brighter Nights does not guarantee leads, rankings, or revenue.

3. Fee

3.1 Partner pays a Fee equal to a percentage of Gross Lighting Revenue in marginal tiers per contract year: 7% of the first $250,000; 6% of the next $250,000; 5% of the next $250,000; 4% of the next $250,000; 3% above $1,000,000. Tiers reset each contract year.
3.2 If Partner elects a Committed Term of 2 or 3 years, each percentage in 3.1 is reduced by 0.5 points for each year beyond the first, as shown in Exhibit B, for the whole Committed Term.
3.3 Monthly report. By the 10th of each month Partner reports the prior month's Gross Lighting Revenue in the Dashboard, job by job, with customer name, address, product footage, and amount, and the owner certifies the report as complete and accurate. Brighter Nights invoices on receipt. Payment is due within 10 days by ACH or card on file. Late amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum lawful rate, whichever is less, plus reasonable collection costs.
3.4 All lighting work counts. Gross Lighting Revenue includes every lighting job performed in the Territory by Partner, its owners, employees, subcontractors, any affiliate or entity under common ownership or control, or any other trade name Partner uses, whether the lead came from the Site or anywhere else, and whether paid by card, check, financing, cash, or trade. Partner will not route lighting work through another person, entity, or brand, and will not split, defer, or relabel invoices, to reduce the Fee.
3.5 Books and records. Partner keeps complete and accurate books of all lighting work in one accounting system, issues an invoice through that system for every lighting job, collects Site booking deposits through the payment processor connected to the Site, and keeps invoices, contracts, bank and processor statements, and distributor purchase records for three years after the term. On request Partner provides a monthly sales report from its accounting system within 5 business days.
