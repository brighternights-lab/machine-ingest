# Pool enclosure sale, the three-system recommendation

**Topic.** How Austen recommends products for a pool enclosure job
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Direct from Austen Stucki (reply to a Screen Factory Florida inquiry).

**The three systems that go on a pool enclosure sale.** When a contractor is quoting pool cages and lanais, Austen's recommendation is a three-system package:

1. **Pool Enclosure Track (24V)** mounts on the cage beams.
2. **Sconce Lighting (12V)** up & down wall wash for the vertical posts.
3. **Roofline Track (24V)** permanent fascia, gable and eave lighting for the attached home, sold by the foot.

Every system is a complete, ready-to-install low-voltage kit with no 110V conversion.

**Why these three together.** The pool enclosure track takes care of the cage structure itself. The sconce wall wash handles the vertical posts and gives the enclosure dimension. The roofline track ties the attached home into the same ecosystem so the whole property reads as one lighting system from one app.

**Positioning line Austen uses.** "Pool cages and lanais are our core market, so [the contractor's shop] is exactly the kind of partner we built our systems for." Over 20 years in the industry, having installed just about every RGB product on the market in the U.S. and abroad, Austen became a manufacturer because he saw the value of a true whole-property ecosystem backed by real training and support, and no other manufacturer was delivering it.

# Pool Enclosure Track, full field specs

**Topic.** Pool Enclosure Track specification
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Direct from Austen Stucki.

The field specs for Pool Enclosure Track in Austen's own words.

- **Material:** Powder-coated 6063-T5 aluminum from the same mill and paint as enclosure framing.
- **Track lengths:** 10 ft and 22 ft.
- **Finishes:** Black, bronze, white, matched to the cage.
- **LEDs:** RGB integrated at 12-inch spacing: 16 million+ colors, tunable white, dimmable, individually addressable, 50,000-hour rated.
- **Patterns:** 180+ preset patterns and scenes.
- **Ingress protection:** IP67 strands and bulbs; IP68 controller and power supply.
- **Connections:** Factory-terminated twist-on waterproof connectors, zero field splicing.
- **Scale:** Up to 1,000 ft per controller.
- **Kit sizes:** 100 to 300 ft.
- **Kit includes:** Bluetooth controller, 200W power supply, coax lead with connectors, T-connectors, waterproof enclosure box, and signal amplifiers. **Same kit on Roofline.**

**Design discipline.** Design to 290 to 300 lights on a 200W power supply (per L4-COURSE-M02-L01), never to max. Signal amplifiers drop in after every 300 lights or on any long run (per L4-COURSE-M02-L04).

# Sconce Lighting, full field specs

**Topic.** Sconce specification and bulb-architecture positioning
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Direct from Austen Stucki.

**Kit includes:** solid brass fixtures, HyperLux MR16 RGBW bulbs, transformer (150W; 300W on the 12-pack), 100 ft of 14/2 wire, Bluetooth mesh controller and all mounting hardware.

**Material.** Solid brass under a UV-stable powder coat, salt-spray tested 500+ hours. Brass doesn't rust, so it's ideal for coastal properties.

**Finishes.** Matte Black, Antique Bronze, Pure White.

**Electrical.** 12V AC or DC, dual up & down throw.

**Output.** 600 lumens at 5.5W on **every color, not just white**. (The HyperLux architecture puts full wattage into whichever color is on, instead of splitting it across R/G/B. See L4-VOICE-PRODUCT-DIFFERENTIATION for the 2-3x brighter positioning.)

**Color and scenes.** 16 million colors, tunable white (2000K to 7500K), 100+ scenes, dimmable.

**Life and ingress.** 40,000-hour bulb life, IP64.

**Kit sizes.** 4, 8 or 12 fixtures.

**Why MR16 drop-in and not an integrated module.** The sconce uses a drop-in MR16 bulb rather than an integrated module **by design**. Each bulb is individually addressable, so every sconce can be a different color, even one color up and another down. Bulbs swap by hand with no tools.

**Firmware forward-protection.** Bulbs and all systems receive firmware updates, so new features reach the customer without new hardware. That protects both the homeowner and the contractor.

# The Wi-Fi callback positioning

**Topic.** How Austen pitches the Bluetooth-mesh advantage over Wi-Fi-based systems
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Direct from Austen Stucki.

**The headline Austen uses.** "Wi-Fi is the number one callback in permanent lighting."

**The economic framing.** Wi-Fi-dependent systems, such as Gemstone, typically send crews back to the property **three to five times a year**, and a **$20,000 to $50,000 job can be taken down by a $30 router**. The pitch lands because the contractor sees the service load that waits on the back end of every Wi-Fi install.

**Why BNLT isn't exposed to it.** BNLT systems run on their own Bluetooth mesh, fixture to fixture, so router swaps and internet outages have no effect on the lights.

**App stack.**
- App control on iOS, Android and web, with a 200 ft range.
- Color, dimming, scenes, zones, holiday modes and sunset scheduling.
- Accessories: Bluetooth remote, astro timer, music-sync controller, signal repeater.
- Optional Wi-Fi gateway for **remote access only** — never required for the lights to run.

**The one-liner for the field.** "Our systems run on their own Bluetooth mesh — router swaps and internet outages have no effect on the lights. The Wi-Fi gateway is optional, for remote access only."
