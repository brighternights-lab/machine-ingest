# Landscape Lighting system, the public spec

**Topic.** Landscape full architectural spec (three bulb platforms)
**Audience.** Everyone L2 and above (public page)
**Voice.** Marketing-page copy, current rev6.

**Architecture.** 12V low voltage throughout. Solid brass fixtures on MeshTek Bluetooth mesh — every fixture is a node that relays to the next. Scales from a 20-fixture yard to thousands of fixtures on one network.

**Three bulb platforms (the whole range).**

- **MR16 Up Light** — 5W, 500 lumens, 40° beam. Palms up to 25 ft, sides of home, front elevation. The everyday workhorse.
- **PAR36 Up Light** — 16W, 1500 lumens, 10°/24°/60°/120° beam. Three times the output of the MR16. For specimen trees taller than 25 ft (royal palms, mature oaks). Osram RGBW.
- **G4 Bi-Pin Pathway** — 2W, 200 lumens, 120° beam. Pathways, walkways, garden beds, low borders. Wide soft glare-free pool of light underfoot.

**Why drop-in bulbs matter.** The bulb is the product. Every fixture takes a replaceable bulb — a failure years from now is a 60-second swap with the fixture still in the ground. (Most premium landscape brands seal the LED into the fixture, which turns a dead light into an excavation.)

**Finish & material.** Solid brass throughout — NOT brass-plated, NOT brass-coated aluminum. UV-stable powder coat over the brass. 500+ hours of salt-spray testing. **Brass is the only metal in this category that survives coastal and poolside exposure long-term.**

**Environmental.** IP64-rated fixtures with O-ring seals. Verified through 500+ hours of salt-spray testing.

**Mounting.** Brass stakes for planting beds. Surface and hardscape mounting for walls, columns, pavers. Fixtures aim and lock, so a mower or storm does not re-aim the lighting.

**Wiring.** Direct-burial-rated low-voltage landscape cable. Gauge selected to run length so the last fixture is as bright as the first. Buried below mow line and routed clear of future planting.

**Connectors.** Waterproof low-voltage connectors, direct burial and wet-location rated. No exposed splices in a planting bed.

**Control.** Every fixture individually addressable (not zones, not a six-zone remote). Bluetooth mesh fixture-to-fixture. Wi-Fi optional, only for off-property control. Astronomical timer runs sunrise/sunset without a schedule to maintain. Firmware updates propagate through the mesh.

**Accessories.**
- **The Repeater** — Lets the system act without a phone present. Required for scheduling. Sealed outdoor enclosure, waterproof connectors.
- **The Astro Timer** — Line voltage, four weatherproof switched outlets. Runs on sunrise/sunset for your date and location, shifts with them all year.

**Commercial.** Commercial floods at 50W, 120W, 180W, 400W run on the same network and the same app as the residential landscape line.

**Retrofit path.** HyperLux drop-in bulbs replace standard MR16/PAR36/G4 lamps in existing fixtures. Where a property's existing housings take a standard lamp, the bulbs go into the fixtures already in the ground. No trenching, no re-wiring. Caveat: several premium brands seal the LED into the fixture — those housings cannot take a drop-in bulb from anyone.

# HyperLux, the brightness story the public gets

**Topic.** HyperLux drive-electronics positioning for homeowners
**Audience.** Everyone L2 and above (public page)
**Voice.** Marketing-page copy.

**The headline.** "3× Brighter. By Design."

**The flaw in standard RGB bulbs.** Every conventional RGB bulb shares the same hidden flaw: it can only drive one-third of its rated wattage into any single color. Red/green/blue each carry about a third of the load. Ask for pure red → about 200 lumens → washed out from any distance.

**What HyperLux does.** Drive electronics reengineer that limit. Every color gets the bulb's full rated wattage. The white channel layers in to **deepen saturation rather than dilute it**.

**On the MR16 bulb (sconce + landscape).**
- Pure red: standard ~2W, ~200 lumens, washed out → HyperLux 5.5W, 600 lumens, saturated
- Pure blue: standard ~2W, ~200 lumens, weak → HyperLux 5.5W, 600 lumens, deep and vivid
- Color + white: standard channels compete, color dies → HyperLux all channels stack, color glows
- From 50 ft: standard color disappears outdoors → HyperLux color reads cleanly at distance

**Why this matters outdoors.** A fixture at the base of a tree has to deliver color at the canopy. A bulb running at a third of its rating does not have the reach. HyperLux halves the problem: full wattage per color is what lets a PAR36 at ground level still deliver saturated color 30+ feet up a mature palm.

**The platform spans form factors.** HyperLux lives in the lamp, not the housing. MR16, PAR36, G4 bi-pin — all three bulb form factors ship with HyperLux. The housing outlives multiple generations of the bulb inside it.

**Disclosure.** HyperLux brightness figures are manufacturer-reported, not independently tested by BNLT.

# Warranty, what the website currently publishes

**Topic.** Current published warranty copy across all 5 product pages
**Audience.** Everyone L2 and above (public page)
**Voice.** Marketing-page copy.
**Doctrine.** Website registration window (90 days) is WRONG. Defer to BRI-484 60-day FINAL ruling.

**Published across every product page, verbatim.**

- Roofline, Soffit, Pool Enclosure: "10 years on the track/fixture, light strands, connectors and amplifiers. 3 years on the power supply, controller and Wi-Fi gateway (controller extendable to 5)."
- Sconce: "10 years on fixture and transformer. 3 years on the MR16 HyperLux bulb (40,000 hour rated)."
- Deck/Dock: "10 years on the Cleat fixture and coaxial lead lines. 3 years on the power supply and controller."
- Landscape: "Limited lifetime warranty on solid brass landscape fixtures."

**The 90-day language that appears across every page.**
> "Registering your system within 90 days of installation activates the coverage."

**The 60-day FINAL ruling (BRI-484).** Registration window is 60 days from install. FINAL. The L4 Genie must answer 60 days and never 90.

**Open fix.** KBG-10 — update warranty copy site-wide to 60 days to match the ruling.

**Where the Genie should look.** L4-RULE-WARRANTY-MAPPING for the ruled answer. Never cite the live website copy on this point without the doctrine_note correction.
