# Contractor pricing is a certification benefit, not a verification benefit

**Topic.** Which tier sees contractor pricing
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Settled ruling that supersedes earlier reading.

**The ruling (verbatim).** "BRI-288's L2 reading superseded; L2 refuses all pricing. Pricing lives at L4."

**What changed.** BRI-682 (2026-08-27) read L2 Verified Contractor as the tier that sees pricing after ID+EIN or 15-min call. BRI-807 (2026-09-16) is Austen's own later ruling and reverses that reading: L2 is the identity-verification floor; L4 is the pricing floor.

**The L1-L5 ladder, final.**
- L1 Public homeowner — no pricing. Never.
- L2 Enrolled contractor (ID + EIN verified via Sunbiz, or 15-min phone call per BRI-682) — identity verified, pricing REFUSED. Can see non-pricing contractor content (install guides, calculators, course lessons that are L2-tier).
- L3 Distributor — distributor-tier content and pricing (separate corpus, not L4's concern).
- L4 Certified contractor (Masters of Lighting Academy complete) — gets contractor pricing. Also gets homeowner leads (per L4-RULE-LEAD-FUNNELLING) and the full L4 Genie.
- L5 Certified distributor rep (Masters of Supply Academy complete) — gets everything L4 has plus distributor-rep content.

**What this means for the Genie.** If a user asks "what's the contractor price on X?" and the ladder places them at L2, the Genie refuses pricing and explains that pricing lives at L4 (certification). If the ladder places them at L4 or L5, the Genie answers with contractor price and MSRP, never distributor cost (BRI-434 Amendment 3 still holds).

**Supersession of L4-RULE-CONTRACTOR-VETTING-FLOW (Round 7 doc).** The Round 7 doc described BRI-682's two-track flow (Fast Track ID+EIN, Slow Track 15-min call) as unlocking "contractor pricing access" at L2. That is now wrong. The two-track flow still exists — it still gates L2 enrollment — but what L2 unlocks is L2-tier CONTENT, not pricing. Pricing requires the paid Academy and L4 certification.

**Why this matters commercially.** This closes BRI-388's commercial-model loop. BNLT trains contractors but does not sell them product (products go through distributors). The ONE thing BNLT sells contractors is the Masters of Lighting Academy at $1,000. Making pricing a certification benefit means the Academy earns its price — pay $1,000, you can quote jobs against real BNLT pricing instead of blind-quoting MSRP.

**Phrasing for the Genie when refusing pricing to L2.** "Contractor pricing is unlocked by Masters of Lighting Academy certification. The Academy is $1,000 and includes [course content], plus pricing access, plus the full L4 Light Genie, plus homeowner leads from BNLT. The two-track identity verification you completed enrolls you as an L2 contractor and unlocks non-pricing content (install guides, calculators, product references). The next step for pricing is the Academy." Never answer with a price for an L2 user. Never imply L2 ever got pricing in the past.

# Warranty component mapping, every component with its term

**Topic.** The complete component-by-component warranty term map
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Settled ruling of record.

**The map.**

| Component | Term |
| --- | --- |
| Track | 10 years |
| Light strands | 10 years |
| 30mm lights | 10 years |
| Soffit components | 10 years |
| Deck components | 10 years |
| Fixtures | 10 years |
| Connectors | 10 years |
| T-connectors | 10 years |
| Signal amplifiers | 10 years |
| Wire / lead line | 10 years |
| **12V transformer** (landscape, sconce) | **10 years** |
| **24V power supply** (roofline, pool enclosure, soffit, deck) | **3 years** |
| Controller | 3 years |
| Wi-Fi gateway | 3 years |
| **Signal repeater** | 3 years |
| Bulbs (MR16, PAR36, G4, A19) | 3 years (5 with upgrade) |
| Labor | NOT covered |

**The 24V question is resolved.** BRI-484's issue text flagged the 24V power supply as UNRULED. The published MOLA M01 L1.2c warranty-registration-guide.pdf (2026-10-01) states it directly: "The 24V power supply (roofline, pool enclosure, soffit, deck): 3 years." That is now the term of record. KBG-08 closed.

**The 12V vs 24V split.** 12V transformer = 10 years. 24V power supply = 3 years. These are different components with different terms. Say "the 12V transformer" or "the 24V power supply" — never "transformer" or "power supply" alone. A homeowner comparing the Sconce page against the Roofline page must see two different components rather than one component with two answers.

**Live /warranty/ page is wrong (BRI-1040).** The /warranty/ page on brighternights.com still lists WiFi gateway and signal repeater in the 10-year bucket. Canon rules both at 3 years. If a contractor references the live page, correct them to this map and cite BRI-484.

**Repeater, specifically.** The signal repeater is 3 years, not 10 years. This matches the Round 7 ruling (L4-RULE-WARRANTY-EXTENSION) that put the repeater in the 3-year upgrade bucket.

**Registration window.** 60 days from install. FINAL (BRI-484 restated 2026-10-02, BRI-793 canonical). Never 90 days. Never ask. See L4-VOICE-WARRANTY-FIELD-NARRATIVE and L4-PAGE-WARRANTY-STATED for doctrine_note on surfaces that still say 90.

**Transfer.** Transferable once, free, within 90 days of property sale, for the remainder of the original term. (The 90 days here is PROPERTY TRANSFER, not warranty registration — different window, different concept, do not conflate.)

**Advance replacement.** Certified contractors can pull a replacement bulb or controller from authorized distributor shelf stock immediately, and BNLT credits the branch. ON HOLD per the PDF: "the branch-credit process with the distributor is still being finalized" (KNO-161). Standard claim is the fallback.

**Standard claim path.** warranty@brighternights.com or 407-469-7093. Claims go through a 10-business-day review.
