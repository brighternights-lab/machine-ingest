
# Module 8 Lesson 8.4 — Overcoming Objections

**Module.** Sales, estimating and closing system
**Topic.** Sales, estimating, closing
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)

Lesson 8.4: Overcoming Objections (UPGRADED) Objection 1: "It's too expensive" Response: "Totally fair — most people feel that way at first until they see what this actually replaces." No more seasonal installs No more temporary lighting No ongoing setup or teardown "This is a one - time install that you use every day, year - round." "Most homeowners realize it's actually cheaper long - term." Objection 2: "I only want part of the house" Response: "That's actually how a lot of our projects start." "We can break this into phases — start with one section and expand later." Everything integrates No rework required "Most homeowners expand once they see it." Objection 3: "I've seen cheaper options" Response: "You definitely have — there are lower - cost systems out there." "The difference is those systems typically:" Lose brightness when changing colors Require more maintenance Don't have full control Require multiple apps to manage different areas "This is a professional - grade system where everything works together in one place." Objection 4: "I don't need color - changing" Response: "Most homeowners actually use white lighting every day — but here's the difference…" You're not limited to one white You get 12 different white temperature options You can dial in the exact look you want "Color is just a bonus for holidays or events." "What you're really getting is better everyday lighting — all controlled in one system." Objection 5: "I need to think about it" Response: "Of course — this is your home, you should think it through." "Let me ask you this — is there anything specific you're unsure about?" Soft Close Follow - Up "If everything made sense today, what part would you want to start with?"

# Module 8 Lesson 8.5 — Closing the Job

**Module.** Sales, estimating and closing system
**Topic.** Sales, estimating, closing
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)

Lesson 8.5: Closing the Job (REAL SCRIPT) Step 1: Confirm Value "Does everything make sense so far?" "Do you like how this would look on your home?" Step 2: Narrow the Decision "Would you want to do the full property, or start with the front and expand later?" Step 3: Give Direction "Most homeowners choose to start with [front / full system] because it gives the biggest impact." Step 4: The Close "If we got this scheduled, would sooner or later work better for you?" Step 5: Lock It In "Perfect — we'll get you on the schedule and take care of everything." Alternative Close "Which section would you like to move forward with?" Upgrade Close "If we bundle these areas together, you'll get a complete look and save on install time — most homeowners go this route." Urgency Close "We can get this installed before your next event or holiday."
