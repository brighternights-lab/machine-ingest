# Why the BNLT product is different

**Topic.** Product differentiation
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Direct from Austen Stucki.

**Bluetooth mesh, not WiFi.** WiFi driven systems are the number one source of callbacks in this industry. A lot of senior customers do not even have WiFi. BNLT systems run on a Bluetooth mesh, so anyone with a smartphone is good to go. For customers who do not want to use a phone at all, BNLT has a **handheld Bluetooth remote** with about twelve moving patterns plus speed, brightness, and color control.

**No splicing, ever.** The 24V systems (Roofline, Soffit, Deck, Dock, Pool and Screen Enclosure) are completely plug and play. T connectors and three pin coax style lead lines handle every connection. No wire strippers, no electrical tape, no wire nuts. The entire system is waterproof, including the controllers and power supplies.

**Power injection.** No additional power needed until roughly **250 to 300 feet**, and when needed it is a plug in connection, not a splice. Competitors inject every **60 to 90 feet** and splice each one.

**Scale.** Up to **1,000 feet on a single controller**. Multiple controllers sync on one app, so a 5,000 or 10,000 foot commercial property is no problem. Everything can change together or each run can be controlled individually.

**Faster installs.** Contractors switching over are seeing roofline installs run **20 to 30 percent faster** because there is one wire from the controller to the first run of lights, not four, six, or eight.

**Firmware, not new generations.** The hardware stays the same. New patterns, scenes, and controls come through firmware updates, so a customer who buys two years from now has the same technology as the one who bought today. No next generation loop where old product will not sync with new product and has to be ripped out.

**Landscape lighting brightness.** Every RGB landscape bulb on the market splits its wattage across red, green, and blue, so any single color only gets about **a third of the output**. BNLT's bulbs put the full wattage into whichever color is on. That is not 20 or 30 percent brighter, it is **two to three times brighter**, and the color saturation is unreal in comparison.

**Soffit and track lighting.** Three diodes per light with a wide beam angle. Instead of harsh triangle hotspots and shadows, you get a **clean, even wash** that paints the exterior of the home.

**Topic.** Lead flow and distributor referral
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)

Paid advertising starts next year, and getting product stocked at a local ABC Supply branch moves that market up the priority list fast.

# The maintenance program

**Topic.** Mandatory annual maintenance visit
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Direct from Austen Stucki.

This is the play that doubles the business. Tell the homeowner that unlike every other quote they are getting, you include a **mandatory annual maintenance visit**. **Book it on the spot for January or February.**

What the visit covers. A sales rep walks the property, checks the system, runs the **firmware update**, and walks out with the next project. The maintenance visit is the natural moment to pitch the next upsell (landscape in spring, deck in summer, pool enclosure if applicable).

The impact. Contractors doing this right see **30 to 50 percent more business from it** and it costs nothing but a visit.

Why it works. The maintenance visit is both a differentiator at close and a built-in sales touch every year. Most competitors do not do this because their systems are not stable enough to support the visit — WiFi based competitors are reactive to callbacks, not proactive on upkeep.
