# Roofline Lighting system, the public spec

**Topic.** Roofline Track System full architectural spec
**Audience.** Everyone L2 and above (public page)
**Voice.** Marketing-page copy, current rev27.

**Architecture.** 24-volt, spliceless, sealed T-connectors. No field cutting or splicing anywhere in the system.

**Track.** 2-inch aluminum, 3-foot sections. Colors: Black, Bronze, White. LEDs integrated at 12-inch spacing for an even unbroken line.

**System sizes.** 100, 150, 200, 250, 300 ft complete kits. Expandable to 1,000 ft on a single controller.

**Fixtures.** IP67 waterproof 30mm LED. Segment runs in 1, 3, 5, 10 ft.

**Control.** IP68 Bluetooth mesh controller, 4-port or single-port. 24V DC, 200 ft open-air range. 180+ preset patterns. 16 million colors.

**Power.** 200W IP68 waterproof power supply, outdoor-rated.

**Weather.** IP68 controller + IP68 power supply + IP67 light strands/bulbs.

**Signal.** Inline amplifiers keep color and timing consistent across long runs.

**Accessories (shared across all BNLT systems).** Astro timer, Bluetooth remote, music-sync controller, Wi-Fi gateway, signal repeater.

**Energy.** Fixtures engineered to draw about one-third the energy of standard LED fixtures. 200W power supply runs the full system.

**Install position.** Fascia-mounted track, low-voltage runs, waterproof controller and power placement. Certified contractor install only.

**Lifetime.** 50,000-hour rated LEDs.

**Live URL.** brighternights.com/lighting-system/roofline-track-lighting/ (rev27 handoff, BRI-183)

# Pool Enclosure Lighting system, the public spec

**Topic.** Pool Enclosure Track full architectural spec
**Audience.** Everyone L2 and above (public page)
**Voice.** Marketing-page copy, current rev41.

**Architecture.** 24V DC constant voltage. Twist-on waterproof junctions throughout, zero field cutting or splicing. Factory-terminated plug-and-play runs.

**Track.** Powder-coated 6063-T5 architectural aluminum. Same mill, same powder coat as the pool enclosure framing. 10-foot and 22-foot sections. Black, Bronze, White finishes to match the cage. **Guaranteed not to fade faster than the enclosure.** Lights integrated at 12-inch spacing.

**System sizes.** 100, 150, 200, 250, 300 ft complete kits. Expandable to 1,000 ft per controller. Power supply and controller included in every kit.

**Power.** 24V DC constant voltage. 200W IP68-rated power supply included. Inline amplifiers keep color and timing consistent on long runs.

**Connectors.** Pre-installed twist-on waterproof connectors. Gold-pin contacts in threaded gasketed collars that hand-tighten and stay watertight through pool chemistry, salt air, storms. Tool-free, no splicing anywhere.

**Wiring.** 18AWG low-voltage wire. Direct-burial and wet-location rated. Routes invisibly along cage framing.

**Mounting.** Low-profile clips included in every kit. Designed for aluminum enclosure framing. Nearly invisible in daylight.

**LEDs.** RGBW, 16 million+ colors fully dimmable. Tunable whites from warm to cool. 12-inch bulb spacing on all 24V strands. Waterproof 30mm fixtures in 1/3/5/10 ft segment runs. 50,000-hour rated.

**Environmental.** Light strands and bulbs IP67. Controller IP68. Power supply IP68. Sealed for pool chemistry, salt air, storms.

**Control.** BNLT app over Bluetooth. 4-port or single-port controller. 200 ft open-air range. Full-color and tunable-white, schedules, sunset automation, scenes, one-tap holiday modes, 180+ preset patterns.

**Compatibility.** 100% compatible with every BNLT component and accessory. Expand into rooflines, landscape, sconces, and dock lighting under the same app.

# Sconce Lighting system, the public spec

**Topic.** Sconce + MR16 HyperLux bulb full spec
**Audience.** Everyone L2 and above (public page)
**Voice.** Marketing-page copy, current rev39.

**Fixture.** Solid brass body under UV-stable powder coat. Finishes: Matte Black, Antique Bronze, Pure White. **Salt-spray tested 500+ hours.** Brass does not rust, built for coastal properties.

**Dimensions.** Low-profile brass cylinder on low-profile bracket. No visible fasteners, no cast ornament, no plastic lens ring. Mounts to vertical posts and architectural walls at eye level.

**Electrical.** 12V AC or DC. Dual up & down throw simultaneously.

**Bulb (the light engine).** MR16 HyperLux RGBW smart bulb. Drop-in replaceable — bulb swaps by hand with no tools.
- Supply voltage: 10.8-15V DC or AC
- Power: 5.5W
- Lumens: 600 (on every color, not just white)
- White range: 2000K to 7500K
- Beam angle: 40°
- Lifetime: 40,000 hours
- Control: Bluetooth mesh via iOS, Android, web
- IP rating: IP64
- Certifications: FCC, IC

**Why MR16 drop-in by design.** Each bulb is individually addressable on the Bluetooth mesh. Every sconce can be a different color — even one color up and another down within a single fixture. The light engine can be upgraded years from now without touching the fixture, wire, or transformer.

**Kit sizes.** 4, 8, or 12 fixtures. Transformer sized by certified contractor: 150W on 4/8-pack, 300W on the 12-pack.

**Kit includes.** Solid brass sconces (selected finish), MR16 HyperLux RGBW bulbs, waterproof low-voltage transformer, 100 ft of 14/2 wire, Bluetooth mesh controller, all mounting hardware.

**Install time.** About one day for a typical system. Plug-and-play, no splicing.

**HyperLux positioning.** Full 5.5W on every color, not split 3-ways like conventional RGB. Manufacturer-reported ~3× brighter than standard RGB bulbs on a single saturated color. Outdoors at 50 ft, this is "the difference between color you can see and color you cannot."

# Deck & Dock Lighting system, the public spec

**Topic.** Deck & Dock full architectural spec (Cleat deck light)
**Audience.** Everyone L2 and above (public page)
**Voice.** Marketing-page copy, current rev15.

**Fixture.** 24V LED deck light with brushed stainless face. Sits flush in decking, docking, or pavers. Sealed coaxial lead-line connectors.

**Install method.** Single core drill into pavers, composite decking, or wood decking.

**Load rating.** **Drive-over rated.** Set it in a paver run or driveway and park on it.

**Ingress.** IP67 — rated against jetting water, rain, and washdown in a permanent outdoor install.

**Environment.** Specified for the marine environment: dock edges, seawalls, pilings, walkways. Holds up to salt air, rain, washdown, direct sun.

**Connectors.** Sealed coaxial lead lines connect fixture to fixture. Lead lines in 1, 3, 5, 10 ft. **No splicing required.**

**Addressability.** Every fixture individually addressable. One run can carry one color or several.

**Power & control.** 24V low-voltage system. One-port controller. Bluetooth mesh, no Wi-Fi dependency. 16 million colors, 100+ scenes.

**Shared parts.** Lead lines are shared across every 24V BNLT system, so a deck and dock run uses the same parts inventory as roofline, soffit, and pool enclosure.

**Material note.** Review flagged inconsistency between stainless deck fixture and "aluminum track" FAQ language carried over from other systems. The authoritative material is **brushed stainless** on the deck light fixture; aluminum track belongs to roofline + pool enclosure, not deck/dock.
