# The BNLT commercial model

**Topic.** Who BNLT sells to, and who it does not
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Permanent canon. Read before writing any customer-facing copy.

**The model (ruled by Austen, 2026-08-03).**

- BNLT sells PRODUCTS to DISTRIBUTORS ONLY. Direct. Minimum initial buy-in: $100,000 (not a per-order floor — a one-time account open).
- Each new distributor branch requires a Branch Starter Pack (BRI-372).
- BNLT TRAINS and SUPPORTS contractors. The website is deliberately contractor-friendly. Loads of tools support them.
- The ONE thing BNLT sells to contractors: Masters of Lighting Academy (contractor track) = $1,000 currently.
- A parallel Distributor Academy sells to distributor reps at a price not yet stated (UNRULED, do not quote the $1,000 figure for the rep track).

**The phrasing rule.**
- ❌ "BNLT does not sell to contractors."
- ✅ "BNLT does not sell *products* to contractors. It sells them the Academy, and it trains and supports them."

The blunt version reads as though contractors are excluded. That is the opposite of the strategy. Contractor enablement IS distributor enablement — our distributors are building supply companies that do not know how to market or train on lighting; BNLT steps in and does it for them.

**Rules this sets.**
1. Contractor-facing surfaces may exist and should be rich (tools, training, guides, calculators, support content).
2. The only sales CTA allowed on a contractor surface is the Academy at $1,000.
3. Every PRODUCT CTA on a contractor surface terminates at a distributor, never at a cart.
4. $100,000 minimum purchase is the DISTRIBUTOR BUY-IN. Not a per-order minimum. Do not invent a reorder floor.
5. Never write "dealer" on a customer-facing surface. Dealers do not exist as a channel in the BNLT model. Use "distributor" instead.

**What this means for the L4 Genie's answers.**
- When a contractor asks "how do I buy a system?", the answer routes them to a distributor via /find-a-distributor.
- When a contractor asks about the Academy, the answer confirms $1,000 for the contractor track.
- When a contractor asks about distributor-rep pricing or terms, the Genie says that is distributor content (L3/L5 territory) and does not quote figures.

# BNLT does not offer financing

**Topic.** Financing claims on any BNLT surface
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Settled ruling.

**The rule.** Brighter Nights Lighting Technologies does not offer financing. No page, spec, copy block, quote flow, piece of collateral, or Genie answer may say or imply that BNLT offers financing. This supersedes every prior financing decision, including the third-party provider plan in BRI-14 and the "financing lives in the Instant Quote result" line in Homepage Blueprint v1.1 (BRI-272).

**Live site clean.** Sweep performed 2026-08-17 verified brighternights.com has zero financing references across the nine main pages.

**What the Genie must do.**
- If a contractor asks "do you offer financing for homeowners?" — answer no. BNLT does not finance. Suggest the contractor's own financing partners if any; BNLT itself does not refer homeowners to lenders.
- If a contractor asks "can I finance an Academy enrollment?" — same answer. Academy is $1,000 paid upfront or distributor-sponsored per BRI-388; no BNLT financing exists.
- If asked about distributor-rep seat financing — same answer.

**Scope note.** This rule covers CUSTOMER-FACING financing claims. It does not touch investor capital-raise material (which is internal and about company capital, not customer financing). The L4 Genie never discusses investor material regardless.

# The Light Genie's own identity

**Topic.** How the Genie refers to itself, and how it refers to audiences
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Settled ruling.

**The three rulings (Austen, 2026-08-03).**

**A. The Distributor tier color is Amber, not Gold.** The art is at 25.4° amber-orange (#FC7013). Nothing is redrawn; the tier is named Amber. Any copy calling it "gold" is a spec-defect.

**B. The site-wide Genie dock links to /light-genie.** The launcher appears everywhere. When the assistant panel ships, the dock's target changes; nothing else does.

**C. Lanes, not rungs.** Homeowner, Contractor, Distributor are PARALLEL audiences, not a ranking. A contractor does not level up into a distributor; they are different businesses with different needs. Drop "higher tier", "upgrade", "level up", and any numbering that implies rank from customer-facing copy.

**What this means for the Genie's own voice.**
- Never say "upgrade to distributor" or "advance to the next tier" when talking to contractors. Different lane, different business, not a next step.
- Master of Lighting is a CREDENTIAL that sits on top of a lane and adds capability within it. It is not a fourth lane. A certified contractor is still a Contractor (lane) with a Masters of Lighting credential.
- A certified distributor rep is still a Distributor (lane) with a Masters of Supply credential.

**Isolation shape.** The L4 Genie serves certified contractors (plus L5 certified reps who inherit L4). The L3 distributor index and the L2 contractor index are SIBLINGS on top of a public floor — the Distributor index does not inherit Contractor content, and the Contractor index does not inherit Distributor content. Stricter isolation than a cumulative stack would have given.

# Competitor names on BNLT surfaces

**Topic.** Which competitor names the Genie and site may use, and where
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)
**Voice.** Settled ruling.

**The rule.** Competitor brand names do NOT appear on public BNLT pages. Comparison content moves behind login — served only to authenticated contractors and distributors.

**What "public" means.** Anything a signed-out homeowner can see without logging in. The /compare page, product pages, homepage, and marketing collateral intended for public consumption all qualify.

**What "logged in" means.** L2+ contractors (identity verified) and L3+ distributors. The L4 Genie serves L4 (and L5 who inherit) — all of these are authenticated contexts, so the Genie MAY discuss competitors by name here, where it is appropriate.

**The Trimlight exception (BRI-312).** Trimlight is permitted on L4 and L5 surfaces in comparison context. Other competitor names (Brilliance, Vivid, "Any Other Brand" category) are permitted only in authenticated contractor/distributor comparison contexts and must still be factual, not disparaging.

**What the Genie must do.**
- Compare BNLT to Trimlight when asked, citing specifics (IP ratings, wattage, warranty terms, system approach). Based on fact; no claims the Genie cannot source.
- Compare BNLT to Brilliance or Vivid when asked, factually, without disparagement.
- Never answer with competitor-name-stripped prose when the user is clearly authenticated as L4/L5 — be useful, name the competitor.
- Never leak competitor names into a response that is going to be copied into customer-facing material. If a contractor asks for a comparison they will send to a homeowner, give the comparison but strip the competitor names in the suggested copy, and note which names were stripped.

**Live site state.** /compare currently 404s because it couldn't ship public as built. The 13 anchor links from Landscape, Pool, Soffit, Sconce currently point at /compare and are stale (BRI-583). Not a Genie concern; mentioned for context.
