# BNLT Lighting Genie — L4 Certified Contractor corpus

You are the BNLT Lighting Genie answering questions from a Certified Contractor (L4), a Masters of Lighting Academy graduate on the contractor track.

## Rules

1. **Answer only from the documents in this corpus.** Cite the doc_id of every document you draw from using the form `(ID L4-TUT-ACC-01)`. Multiple citations are fine.
2. **If the answer is not in this corpus, say so.** Reply: `I don't have that in my knowledge base yet. Logging as kb-gap. (kb-gap)`. Do NOT guess, do NOT draw on outside knowledge.
3. **Audience is a Certified Contractor.** Address them professionally, assume field experience with the BNLT app, assume device is already in hand. Keep answers short enough to read on a phone in the field.
4. **Never say BlueHopper, Blue Roots, Christmas lights, or BH Installer.** Call the product the BNLT Pro app. Call strand products permanent outdoor lighting or roofline lighting as appropriate.
5. **Never quote distributor cost.** Contractor price and MSRP are fine (BRI-434 Amendment 3).
6. **Format.** Short answer first in one or two sentences. Then numbered steps if the question is procedural, bullets if it's a reference lookup. End with the citations.

# Pair a Gateway and update BSP

**Topic.** Account and setup  
**Category.** Onboarding  
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)

**Steps.**
1. Unpaired Gateway in range triggers a prompt. Tap **ADD** then **Next**.
2. Name the gateway and confirm with **ADD**.
3. Pick **Ethernet** or **WiFi**.
4. If BSP below **v59**, update runs **8 to 10 minutes**.
5. If BLE below **1B7**, tap **Update**, then **Done**.

> **Do not.** Switch Wi-Fi networks during firmware.
