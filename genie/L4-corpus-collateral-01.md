# How a contractor hands off the warranty at the end of a job

**Topic.** The published M01 L1.2c warranty-registration-guide, which is the field script
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4)
**Voice.** Published collateral. This IS the field reference contractors print.

**The headline promise.** The warranty is one of the strongest things you can put in front of a customer, and one of the easiest to get wrong. Everything in the published Limited Warranty. Quote it exactly, point to it in writing, and make sure every customer registers on time.

**Coverage by component (abbreviated — the full map is in L4-RULE-WARRANTY-COMPONENT-MAP).** Track, light strands, 30mm lights, fixtures: 10 years. Connectors, T-connectors, signal amplifiers, signal repeaters: 10 years (NOTE: BRI-484 canon moves repeaters to 3-year; the PDF's 10-year claim on the repeater line is a propagation defect flagged on BRI-1040 — defer to the ruling). Lead line and wire, soffit and deck components: 10 years. 12V transformer (landscape and sconce): 10 years. 24V power supply (roofline, pool enclosure, soffit, deck): 3 years. Controllers and Wi-Fi Gateway: 3 years. Bulbs (MR16, PAR36, G4, A19): 3 years (5 with upgrade). Labor: NOT covered.

**How a customer registers.**
1. At the handoff, give the customer the activation link: brighternights.com/activate-warranty
2. Check the invoice. The INSTALLATION DATE has to be on it, because the 60-day registration window counts from that date.
3. They register within 60 DAYS OF INSTALLATION: contact details, installation date, systems installed.
4. Optional: they add the 5-year upgrade for bulbs and controllers.
5. They review and accept the terms.
6. They save the confirmation for their records.

**The contractor's part as installer.**
1. Record the 16-digit prepaid code from the controller in the job record, with a photo. Needed for support and for transferring the system to the customer.
2. Send the activation link the DAY the install finishes, not later.
3. Explain coverage in plain words using the component table.
4. Keep a copy of the invoice with the installation date in the job record.

**Optional 5-year upgrade pricing (bulbs and controllers, 3 → 5 years).**
- MR16 bulb: $10 per bulb
- PAR36 bulb: $15 per bulb
- A19 bulb: $10 per bulb
- G4 bulb: $5 per bulb
- Controller: $50

**When something fails.** Standard claim: contact warranty@brighternights.com or 407-469-7093. Claims go through a 10-business-day review. Advance replacement: certified contractors can pull a replacement bulb or controller from authorized distributor shelf stock, and BNLT credits the branch. (ON HOLD — branch-credit distributor process still being finalized, KNO-161.) Labor: not covered. Plan service labor in your own pricing.

**Fine print in plain words.**
- Transfer: Transferable ONCE, free, within 90 days of the property sale, for the remainder of the original term.
- Implied warranties: Limited to the written term. Some states give rights beyond it, and those still apply.
- Venue: Middle District of Florida, Orlando Division, and the Circuit Court in and for Seminole County, Florida.
- Contact: warranty@brighternights.com · 407-469-7093.

**Do's and don'ts (contractor-facing).**
- ✓ Point to the written warranty.
- ✓ Say the component and its term exactly.
- ✓ Get every customer registered on time.
- ✗ Say "lifetime" or "forever."
- ✗ Promise labor coverage.
- ✗ Compare warranty lengths with other brands (public-facing — allowed in L4-authenticated context per L4-RULE-COMPETITOR-NAMES).

# Contractor onboarding and gallery access, in plain words

**Topic.** The first-steps guide BNLT sends to a new contractor
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) — the Light Genie Master gets the same material contractors receive at onboarding
**Voice.** Published collateral. Welcoming.

**Who it is for.** A contractor who reached out because they want to add permanent outdoor lighting to what they offer. The local Outdoor Solutions rep is their point of contact; a Masters of Light rep stands behind them.

**A complete whole-property system.** BNLT is one connected lighting ecosystem for the entire property, not a single product line. Lines that work together on one app and one mesh network: Roofline, Soffit, Landscape (MR16, PAR36, G4 Bi-Pin), Pool & Screen Enclosure, Sconce, Deck, Dock. (This onboarding PDF counts Deck and Dock separately; canon count is six — see doctrine_note above.) One homeowner becomes one project that can cover the whole house, the yard, and the water.

**Engineered on 24V.** The 24V family runs on 24 volts (contrasted with 12V landscape). Longer cable runs, more consistent brightness across a full property, and added electrical headroom that 12V systems do not have. HyperLux landscape drives full wattage on every color channel, so the output stays true and bright instead of dimming when colors mix.

**Training and field support.** You are never on your own. Before your first job you get trained on the product and the install. On your first installs the Outdoor Solutions rep is there, and a Brighter Nights field specialist is there in person, to make sure it goes smoothly.

**Coming soon.** Online courses accessible from a personal device, support on demand whichever system you are installing. (This is the Masters of Lighting Academy and the published M01-M11 PDF modules — now largely shipped per L4-TUTORIAL and L4-COURSE docs.)

**What you can send homeowners from day one.** Prepurchase gallery: sales sheets, flyers, catalogs, product brochures. Send them straight to homeowners to help win the sale, even before your first order.

**The photo and video gallery you can white-label (after first purchase).** Once you place your first purchase you unlock the full photo and video library: thousands of real project images and videos you can white-label and market as your own work. First purchase can be anything, including an investment in your own property (for example a roofline system on your own home). Place that first order; the full gallery is yours.

**Three levels of gallery access.**
- Prepurchase Access: New contractors, before a first order. Sales sheets, flyers, catalogs, product brochures.
- Contractor Access: Contractors, after a first purchase. Everything above, plus the full white-label photo and video gallery.
- Distributor Access: Reps and distribution partners. Distributor-level product, pricing, and program pages.

**Registration URL.** https://gallery.brighternights.com/registration/

# Six systems, two families

**Topic.** How BNLT's six systems split into two engineering families and what each family carries
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4)
**Voice.** Field reference, published collateral.

**The headline rule.** Every product BNLT makes belongs to one of two families, and the family decides the rules: how it is powered, how it is wired, whether it is ever spliced. Know the family before planning anything.

**The six systems.**

| System | Family | Runs on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roofline track | 24V splice-less | Four-Port Bluetooth Controller |
| Pool enclosure track | 24V splice-less | One-Port Controller |
| Soffit | 24V splice-less | One-Port Controller |
| Deck and Dock | 24V splice-less | One-Port Controller |
| Landscape (MR16 uplights, PAR36 floods, G4 pathway) | 12V direct-burial | 12V transformer, Bluetooth mesh bulbs |
| Sconce | 12V direct-burial | 12V transformer, Bluetooth mesh bulbs |

**24V systems kit contents.** Controller, 200 W power supply, coax lead line with male and female connectors, T-connectors, waterproof enclosure box, signal amplifiers. The DECK kit ships WITHOUT the enclosure box and T-connectors.

**12V landscape systems kit contents.** Solid-brass fixtures, HyperLux RGBW bulbs, 150 W transformer (300 W on the Sconce 12-pack and PAR36 8-and 12-packs), 100 ft of 14/2 wire, Bluetooth mesh controller.

**Works with both families.** Signal repeater (adds about 200 feet of open-air range each), Astro Timer, Wi-Fi Gateway, Bluetooth remote, music controller.

**One app.** Every fixture on the property meshes on the same network and shows up in the same app.

**The hard rule.** Roofline rules never carry over to Landscape, and Landscape rules never carry over to Roofline. 24V splice-less family and 12V direct-burial family are different in how they are powered, routed, and connected. Keep them separate in your head.

# Module 1 field reference, the five standing questions and the standards

**Topic.** The five questions a certified contractor carries into every install, plus the connection standards
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4)
**Voice.** Field reference, published collateral.

**Families and controllers.**

| Family | Systems | Power and control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 24V splice-less | Roofline, pool enclosure, soffit, deck | Four-Port (roofline) or One-Port, 200 W power supply |
| 12V direct-burial | Landscape, pathway, sconce | Transformer, Bluetooth mesh bulbs |

**The standards.**

| Standard | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Connections | Male end TOWARD the controller. Tab to notch, press first, twist second. |
| Splicing | Never on 24V. Grease-filled waterproof wire nuts on 12V. |
| Power loading | Every power supply and transformer loaded to **85 percent, never more**. |
| Accuracy | Only say what you can back up in writing. |

**The five standing questions (carry into every install).**
1. **Goal.** What is the customer actually trying to light? What is the look they are going for?
2. **Family.** 24V splice-less or 12V direct-burial? The family decides the rules.
3. **Route and connections.** Where does the lead line go? Where does the power supply land? Male-end-toward-controller for every connection, press-and-twist.
4. **Staging.** How does the install order go? What gets powered up first? Where does the controller get mounted?
5. **Handoff.** The 16-digit code recorded with a photo. The activation link sent the DAY of install. The customer registered within 60 days.

**Why the 85 percent rule matters.** Every power supply and transformer loaded to 85 percent and never more. This protects the component's lifetime and leaves headroom for a repeater, a music controller, or an additional fixture added later without re-sizing the power supply.
