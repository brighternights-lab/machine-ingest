
# Module 11 — Ecosystem sales and pricing mastery

**Topic.** Ecosystem sales and pricing mastery
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)

Module 11: ECOSYSTEM SALES & PRICING MASTERY How to Sell Bigger Jobs, Price with Confidence, and Build a High - Profit Lighting Business ----------------------------------------- FINAL POSITIONING This is the module that separates installers from earners. Anyone can install lights. Very few can: walk onto a property see the full opportunity price it correctly and close a $10K – $25K project with confidence This is where everything you've learned becomes revenue. ----------------------------------------- CORE RULE: SELL THE ECOSYSTEM You do not sell: roofline lighting pool enclosure lighting landscape lighting You sell: THE TRANSFORMATION OF THE ENTIRE PROPERTY ----------------------------------------- AVERAGE CONTRACTOR THINKING 150 ft roofline → $4,500 job Install → leave ----------------------------------------- MASTER OF LIGHT THINKING Same house becomes: Roofline Lighting → $4K – $6K Pool Enclosure Lighting → $4K – $7K Landscape Lighting → $3K – $9K Sconces → $2K – $6K Soffit Lighting → $2K – $5K TOTAL PROJECT: $12K – $25K+ Same house. Different mindset. ----------------------------------------- UNDERSTANDING CSRP (CONTRACTOR SUGGESTED RETAIL) Your pricing sheet is not a suggestion. It is a system for protecting your margins and your market. Examples: Roofline Systems: 100 ft → $3K – $4K 150 ft → $4K – $6K 200 ft → $5K – $7K Pool Enclosure Systems: 100 ft → $3K – $4K 200 ft → $5K – $7K 300 ft → $7K – $9K Landscape Lighting: MR16 8 - pack → $3K – $4K PAR36 12 - pack → $9K – $12K Sconces: 8 - pack → $4K – $6K Soffit Lighting: 12 - pack → $5K – $7K ----------------------------------------- WHAT CSRP REALLY MEANS CSRP protects: your profit your time your reputation the entire market If you consistently sell below CSRP, you are not being competitive. You are weakening your business. ----------------------------------------- MARGIN REALITY Example: 150 ft system cost ≈ $2,200 Typical sale ≈ $4K – $6K That is: 50% – 70%+ margin potential 10 jobs per month: ≈ $25K – $30K+ gross profit This is a business — not a side job. ----------------------------------------- THE 3 - TIER PRICING SYSTEM (MANDATORY) Never give one option. ----------------------------------------- OPTION 1: ENTRY Single system (roofline or pool) Example: $4,000 – $5,000 ----------------------------------------- OPTION 2: RECOMMENDED (YOU GUIDE HERE) Two systems combined Example: $8,000 – $12,000 ----------------------------------------- OPTION 3: FULL ECOSYSTEM Complete property transformation Example: $15,000 – $25,000+ ----------------------------------------- WHY THIS WORKS Customers don't choose the cheapest. They choose: what feels complete what feels intentional what solves the whole problem ----------------------------------------- HOW TO WALK A PROPERTY (REAL SALES FLOW) STEP 1: LEAD WITH VISION Do NOT start with price. Say: "Most homeowners start with one area… but what really transforms the home is tying everything together." ----------------------------------------- STEP 2: BUILD THE PROJECT IN THEIR MIND Physically point: "Roofline here" "Pool enclosure here" "Landscape across this line" "Sconces here" "Soffit here" You are designing — not quoting. ----------------------------------------- STEP 3: ANCHOR HIGH Say: "Most full - property projects land between $12K – $20K depending on size." Now: $5K feels small $10K feels reasonable $15K feels premium but justified ----------------------------------------- CLOSING WITHOUT DISCOUNTING Never say: "I can go cheaper" "I'll match that price" Instead say: "We're not the cheapest system — we're the one you don't have to redo." ----------------------------------------- SELL VALUE USING YOUR ADVANTAGES No splicing (roofline, pool, soffit, deck) Waterproof connections Faster installs Fewer callbacks One app for the entire property You are selling: reliability + simplicity + long - term performance ----------------------------------------- THE REAL MONEY: EXPANSION & MAINTENANCE PROGRAM Most contractors think the job ends after installation. Masters of Light know: The real business begins AFTER the install. ----------------------------------------- THE MAINTENANCE PROGRAM (YOUR REVENUE ENGINE) Every installation should lead to a structured return. Not a "maybe." Not "if they call." A system. Position it like this: "We come back to make sure everything is performing perfectly, update your system if needed, and help you expand the lighting over time." ----------------------------------------- WHAT A MAINTENANCE VISIT INCLUDES System performance check Connection inspection App / control walkthrough refresh Scene updates or seasonal adjustments System optimization Visual inspection for expansion opportunities ----------------------------------------- WHY THIS MATTERS This is not just maintenance. This is your: upsell opportunity relationship builder referral generator long - term revenue stream ----------------------------------------- THE STRATEGIC ADVANTAGE You are back in front of the decision makers. Both of them. At the same time. In their home. After they've experienced the result. ----------------------------------------- THIS IS WHERE MOST CONTRACTORS LOSE They install… …and disappear. ----------------------------------------- THIS IS WHERE YOU WIN You return when: trust is already built value has been proven emotion is already there Now expansion becomes natural. ----------------------------------------- HOW TO EXECUTE THE UPSALE (EXACT LANGUAGE) Walk the property again. Point to what was discussed. Say: "I remember, Mr. and Mrs. Smith, you mentioned wanting to add landscape lighting around the palms in the backyard… and you also talked about lighting up that back fence." (Pause) "Now that you've seen how everything looks on the front of the home, this is where it really starts to come together." (Then close:) "Should we go ahead and get that started for you?" ----------------------------------------- WHY THIS WORKS You are not selling from scratch. You are: reminding them of THEIR idea reconnecting them to THEIR vision building on an existing YES ----------------------------------------- THE MULTIPLIER EFFECT One job becomes: Initial install → $5K First expansion → +$4K Second expansion → +$6K Future upgrades → ongoing Customer lifetime value: $10K – $20K+ ----------------------------------------- MAKE IT A SYSTEM Every customer gets: scheduled follow - up (30 – 90 days) annual check - in clear expansion path ----------------------------------------- PRICING MISTAKES (ELIMINATE THESE) guessing prices selling per foot instead of systems discounting early only offering one option ignoring CSRP underpricing labor treating jobs as one - offs ----------------------------------------- FINAL IDENTITY SHIFT Old mindset: "I install lights." New mindset: "I design and install full - property lighting systems." ----------------------------------------- CERTIFICATION CLOSE You now understand: the ecosystem the pricing the opportunity The question is no longer: "Can you install it?" The question is: Can you walk onto a property… see the full opportunity… price it confidently… and close it at scale? ----------------------------------------- FINAL LINE Masters of Light don't compete on price. They define the project, control the scope, and own the outcome.
