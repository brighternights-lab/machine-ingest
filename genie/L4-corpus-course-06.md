# Module 10 Lesson 10.2 — Core Accessories

**Module.** Accessories and expansion components
**Topic.** Accessories and expansion components
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4) and Certified distributor rep (L5)

Lesson 10.2: Core Accessories (Available in Packs) Many core components can be purchased separately through distribution. Lead Lines Available in 10 - packs: 1 ft 3 ft 5 ft 10 ft Used to connect and extend lighting runs Signal Amplifiers Available in 10 - packs Used to: Strengthen signal Prevent flickering Maintain system performance Major Advantage (CRITICAL) Signal amplifiers eliminate the need for data injection and home runs What Competitors Do Most competing systems require: Injecting data and power into the line Running a home run from the controller Re - injecting signal approximately every: ~60 lights or ~60 feet This requires: Cutting into the line Splicing wires mid - run Adding complexity to the system What This System Does With signal amplifiers: No data injection required No mid - run splicing No home run lines needed Why This Matters Installers get: Faster installs Cleaner installs Fewer failure points More reliable systems Installer Takeaway Instead of injecting signal and redesigning runs: Add a signal amplifier Keep the system moving T - Connectors Available in 10 - packs: Black White Used to: Split connections Create branches in lighting runs Major Advantage (CRITICAL) T - connectors are completely splice - less What Competitors Require Most systems use 3 - wire or 4 - wire setups and require: 9 to 12 wires to be spliced together at each T - junction Time - consuming Error - prone Difficult in tight spaces What This System Does With Brighter Nights T - connectors: No splicing required No wire twisting No wire nuts No heat shrink needed Why This Matters (CORE PRINCIPLE) Every splice point is a failure point and a variable in the system More splices = more problems More variables = more troubleshooting More connections = more long - term maintenance What That Leads To In traditional systems: Loose connections Water intrusion Signal issues Callbacks and repairs This creates headaches for contractors over time What This System Does By eliminating unnecessary splices: Fewer failure points More consistent performance Easier installs Less long - term maintenance Installer Rule The fewer splice points in your system, the more reliable your install
