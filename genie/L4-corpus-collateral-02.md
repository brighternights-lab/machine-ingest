# Roofline power and kit sizing

**Topic.** The two numbers that decide a roofline job at the take-off
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4)
**Voice.** Field planning sheet, published M02 L2.1.

**The headline rule.** Two numbers decide a roofline job before you ever climb a ladder: the kit size and the power supply count. Get both right at the take-off and install day is just installing.

**Size the kit.**
1. Measure. The full run, every face of the roofline you are lighting.
2. Pad it. Add 10 to 15 percent for corners, peaks, and waste.
3. Round up. To the next kit size: 100, 150, 200, 250, or 300 ft.

**Count the power supplies.**
1. Every 200 W power supply is loaded to 85 percent: **170 usable watts**.
2. Track draws about **0.55 W per foot** at full output, so one power supply covers about **300 ft**.
3. Past 250 ft fed from one end: add a second power supply for power injection.
4. Outlet near the middle: feed from the center, and each half carries half the load.

**Quick reference.**

| Measured | With 10 to 15% | Kit | Power supplies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 120 ft | 132 to 138 ft | 150 ft | 1 |
| 170 ft | 187 to 196 ft | 200 ft | 1 |
| 215 ft | 237 to 247 ft | 250 ft | 1 |
| 260 ft, fed from one end | 286 to 299 ft | 300 ft | 2 |

**Pro tip.** Count power supplies on the property walk, not on install day. Power is part of the take-off.

**Partial KBG-16 resolution.** The 0.55 W/ft figure for Roofline is now PUBLISHED (M02 L2.1). It is one of the twelve numbers BRI-367 tracks. Still open for the other systems (Soffit, Pool Enclosure, Deck/Dock, Landscape, Sconce) and for the other categories (mesh hop in-field, non-controller-bulb warranty terms, transformer VA sizing).

# Corners are what the customer looks at from the driveway

**Topic.** Which pieces get the 45° cut on inside and outside corners, by track profile
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4)
**Voice.** Field reference, published M02 L2.2.

**Which pieces get the 45.**

| Track | Inside corner | Outside corner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two-piece | Bottom piece only; the base plate hides the top joint | Same 45, opposite direction |
| J-channel | Both pieces; nothing underneath hides a joint | Same 45, opposite direction |

**Tools and finish.**
- **Snips or saw.** Roofline two-piece and J-channel cut with metal snips or a miter saw.
- **Angle finder.** Pitch varies. Read the real angle at every corner instead of assuming 45.
- **Prefab corners.** Use them wherever the kit has one: same seamless look, no cutting.
- **Finish.** Tight, no gap, filed smooth before you fasten.

**Common mistake.** Cutting only one piece on J-channel. With nothing underneath, the seam shows from the ground.

**Pro rule.** Know which track you are holding before you decide how many cuts the corner needs. Every corner will read clean from the ground.

# Catch problems while measuring, not on the ladder

**Topic.** Everything a certified contractor checks before the first base plate goes up
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4)
**Voice.** Field checklist, published M02 L2.3.

**Fascia.**
- Clean.
- Dry.
- Structurally sound. Soft or cracked fascia is a stop: quote the repair separately or hand it off.

**Route and power.**
- Route walked end to end.
- Outlet found and GFCI tested.
- Controller box within 30 ft of lead line of the first run.
- Power supplies counted.

**Hide-the-wire plan.**
- Inside the track with the lights first.
- Downspout and soffit where the house gives you a path.
- Long runs: wire clips, a channel cover over the whole run, or both.

**Staged on the ground.**
- Controller, power supply, and adapters.
- Lead line lengths, T-connectors, amplifiers.
- Track, lights, prefab corners.

**Pro rule.** Every item on this list gets checked BEFORE the first base plate goes up. Problems caught at measurement are 10 minutes; problems caught on a ladder are a return trip.

# How a roofline system goes together

**Topic.** The connection order from outlet to last light, and how the build changes across properties
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4)
**Voice.** Install reference, published M02 L2.4.

**Kit facts.**
- **4 ports** on the Four-Port Bluetooth Controller — any one works for a first run.
- **30 ft** max lead line to the first run.
- **5 ft** or less between amplifiers.

**The connection order.**
200 W power supply → Four-Port Bluetooth Controller → 3-pin to 3-pin adapter (any port) → Signal amplifier → Lead line, up to 5 ft → Signal amplifier → Lead line, if needed → First run of lights.

**The rules that never change.**

| Rule | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Controller box | The power supply and controller mount together inside it, no more than 30 ft of lead line from the first run of lights. |
| Signal | First amplifier directly out of the controller, then every 5 ft or less. Extra amplifiers never hurt. |
| Direction | Every male end points TOWARD the controller. |
| Jumps | Every jump to another level or dormer starts its lead line with a signal amplifier. Never a splice. |

**How it changes on different properties.**

- **Single-story ranch.** Usually one continuous run, one port, one power supply. Short lead line from the box to the first corner.
- **Two-story with gables and dormers.** A signal amplifier at the start of every jump's lead line. Where a ladder can't reach safely, rent a lift and put it in the quote.
- **Runs past 250 ft.** Feed from the center, or add a second power supply for power injection. Count it at the take-off.
- **Multiple zones.** Use ports 1 to 4 as design zones: **20 unique lights each before the pattern repeats**.
- **Commercial storefront.** Lift, barricaded work zone, site rules, and a certificate of insurance on file.
- **Center-fed layouts.** When the outlet lands mid-run, split the run both directions from the box and one power supply covers more.

**Pro tip.** Walk to the ground after each section. If you can see a wire, the section isn't finished.

**Specs new to the corpus here (partial KBG-16).** 30 ft max lead line, 5 ft amplifier spacing, 20 unique lights per zone before pattern repeats. These are three of the twelve numbers BRI-367 tracks for roofline specifically.

# Two-piece vs J-channel

**Topic.** How to pick the roofline track profile and how each one mounts
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4)
**Voice.** Profile guide, published M02 L2.5.

**Headline.** Either track profile can be used for most roofline applications. The fascia in front of you and the look you want decide which one is the better call, and each one mounts its own way.

**Side by side.**

| Factor | Two-piece track | J-channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best on | Standard fascia | Fascia that hangs down |
| Mounting | Base plate first, track snaps on | Up through the light hole, catch the lip |
| Lights | Popped into the track BEFORE it snaps on | Snapped in from BEHIND |
| Inside corner | Cut the bottom piece | Cut both pieces |

**Two-piece sequence.**
1. Mount the base plate through its pre-punched holes, drilling a center hole if you need more than two.
2. Snap the track (lights already in) onto the base plate.
3. Overlap base plate and track so the next section stays seamless.

**J-channel sequence.**
1. Mount up through the light hole, catching the lip on the back side.
2. Come back behind the mounted channel.
3. Snap the lights in from behind.

# The install is finished when it looks finished from the driveway

**Topic.** Post-install walkthrough, test, and handoff
**Audience.** Certified contractor (L4)
**Voice.** Finish checklist, published M02 L2.6.

**The install is finished when.**
- It looks finished from the driveway.
- Lights up in every color.
- The customer knows how to use it.

**From the ground.**
- No visible wire anywhere.
- Every corner reads clean.
- Spacing looks even from where the customer stands.

**Test.**
- Every port and zone lit.
- Full color range, cool white first.
- Controller box closed and sealed.

**Handoff.**
- App walkthrough done.
- Written warranty explained (per L4-COLLATERAL-WARRANTY-REG-GUIDE: 60-day registration window, activation link brighternights.com/activate-warranty, 16-digit code recorded, keep invoice with install date).
- Next phase noted in the job record.

**Why this matters.** The install day is complete only when the handoff items are done. The 16-digit code and the activation link going out the same day are what unlock the warranty-registration window per BRI-484 / L4-RULE-WARRANTY-COMPONENT-MAP.
